Non-negotiable product goals
A. Application Assistant: provide accurate, resume-grounded assistance on Workday, Google Forms, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and other common online job application forms, including custom dropdown arrows, comboboxes, radios, checkboxes, date pickers, dynamic fields, and supported nested frames/shadow roots. NEVER submit any application, click a final action, accept legal declarations, or send information to an employer automatically. If a site's semantics are ambiguous or CAPTCHA/verification appears, stop and ask the user to intervene. Do not bypass site protections.

B. High-paying company research: continuously discover as many legitimate companies as feasible that offer strictly GREATER THAN INR 15,00,000 per year in fixed/base pay to India-eligible fresher/0-2 YOE SWE-1, SDE-1, backend or related software-engineering hires. Bonus, RSUs, joining bonus, stipend and total CTC are NOT base. No company becomes "qualified" merely on a guessed total-comp range. Store the role, geography, compensation type, lower/upper base estimate, currency, observation date, and cited source URL. Separate verified, supported estimate, conflicting and research-needed categories. Include international/remote employers only when India eligibility and pay basis are established; track them separately. Scale the researched database, not fictional entries.

C. Recruiter network: for qualified companies, discover as many relevant PROFESSIONAL recruiting points of contact as is lawful, ToS-compliant and worth contacting: university recruiting, campus TA, technical recruiters, hiring partners, engineering recruitment teams and publicly published recruiting inboxes. Start with company careers/contact sites, job postings, publicly accessible business listings and provider-licensed API data. Optionally use Hunter and Apollo official APIs with user-supplied keys and spending caps. Keep sourced addresses distinct from vendor-found, vendor-inferred, pattern-generated and unverified. Never invent named employees or claim a mailbox was verified from a valid company domain. Do not scrape behind authentication, bypass robots/paywalls/anti-bot controls, harvest personal emails, or run aggressive SMTP VRFY/RCPT mailbox-enumeration campaigns.

D. User-controlled cold outreach CRM: make discovery, prioritization, personalized draft creation, approval, manual/explicit send, follow-up reminders, reply triage and bounce management convenient. Do not mass-send automatically. Sending always requires user approval for recipient and content, and must honor unsubscribe/opt-out, suppression, provider policies and applicable privacy law. Do not rotate identities/accounts to evade limits.

E. Low operating cost: use existing Node/Express, MongoDB, React and Chrome extension where practical; prefer on-demand incremental crawls, caching, source deduplication and local execution. Support a laptop scheduler that runs only while the machine is on, then optional GitHub Actions / a low-cost cloud runner using user-owned budget and credentials.
